Meet Elisa

Therapy should feel like a real conversation.

I specialize in relationships — the ones you have with yourself, and those around you. My focus is helping individuals and couples find deeper clarity, balance, and connection. Sessions blend insight, humor, and levity so you feel secure, confident, and assured in the choices you make. My background in health and fitness shaped my belief that well-being is deeply connected to daily habits, mindset, and communication — a path that led me to therapy as a way to support meaningful, sustainable change.

Our Philosophy
I use a holistic and conversational approach, grounded in mind-body awareness, mindfulness, and practical change — helping you challenge old belief systems, build healthier habits, and move forward with intention.

How we'll work together.

Getting started doesn't have to be complicated. The process is simple, supportive, and designed to move at a pace that feels right for you — from the first hello to the progress you'll see over time.

01

Reach Out

Start with a short introductory call. We'll talk through what brings you here and how I might support you — no pressure, just space to begin.

02

Define Direction

Together, we'll shape a path that fits your needs and pace. It's not about rushing toward a fixed goal, but identifying what truly matters to you.

03

Meet & Reflect

Ongoing one-on-one or couples sessions — online or in-person — offer a consistent space to explore, reflect, and move forward with intention.
